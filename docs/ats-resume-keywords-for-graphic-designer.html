ATS Resume Keywords for Graphic Designer (2026 List)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of getting past applicant tracking systems (ATS) and in front of hiring managers, incorporate relevant keywords into your resume. For graphic designers, this means including terms related to design software, technical skills, and industry-standard tools. Here are the most important ATS resume keywords for graphic designers to include in their applications.

Design Software and Tools

  • Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Acrobat)
  • Sketch and Figma for user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) design
  • Graphic design tools like Canva, Gravit Designer, and GIMP
  • Digital painting and illustration software such as Procreate and Corel Painter
  • Vector graphics editors like Affinity Designer and CorelDRAW

Technical Skills and Formats

  • File formats: JPG, PNG, SVG, EPS, PDF
  • Color modes and models: RGB, CMYK, Pantone
  • Resolution and pixel density: 300 DPI, 72 DPI, retina display
  • Web design and development: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, responsive design
  • Print production: bleed, trim, fold, die-cut

Industry-standard Skills and Certifications

  • Certifications: Adobe Certified Expert (ACE), Adobe Certified Associate (ACA)
  • Design principles: typography, composition, color theory
  • Brand identity and design systems: brand guidelines, style guides
  • Collaboration and project management tools: Asana, Trello, Slack
  • Accessibility and usability: WCAG 2.1, Section 508, user research
