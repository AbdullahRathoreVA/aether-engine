Cover Letter Template for Devops Engineer That Passes ATS

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read
A devops engineer cover letter needs four short paragraphs: why this specific company, the one result that proves you can do the job, how your experience maps to their stated requirements, and a direct close. One page, never more.

If you're struggling to get past the applicant tracking system (ATS) as a devops engineer, it's likely that your cover letter isn't optimized for keywords and formatting. To increase your chances of getting noticed, you need a well-structured cover letter that highlights your technical skills and experience. Here's a template to help you get started.

Introduction and Summary

  • Begin with a formal greeting, addressing the hiring manager by title and last name (if possible)
  • Write a concise summary (approx. 2-3 sentences) that highlights your relevant experience, certifications, and technical skills, such as AWS, Docker, or Kubernetes
  • Mention the job title and how you came across the opportunity, e.g., "I'm excited to apply for the Devops Engineer position at [Company Name], as advertised on [Job Board/Source]">

Technical Skills and Experience

  • Use a bulleted list to outline your technical skills, including:
    • Programming languages: Python, Java, Ruby
    • Cloud platforms: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud
    • Containerization: Docker, Kubernetes
    • Agile methodologies: Scrum, Kanban
  • Describe your experience with devops tools, such as Jenkins, Puppet, or Chef, and how you've used them to automate deployment and scaling
  • Highlight any relevant certifications, such as AWS Certified DevOps Engineer or Google Cloud Certified - Professional Cloud Developer

Best Practices for ATS Optimization

  • Use a standard font, such as Arial or Calibri, and a font size between 10 and 12 points
  • Save your cover letter in a .docx or .pdf format to ensure compatibility with most ATS systems
  • Use keywords from the job description to help your cover letter pass the ATS screening, but avoid over-optimization and focus on showcasing your genuine experience and skills
  • Proofread your cover letter multiple times to ensure error-free formatting and content

Common questions

How many keywords should a devops engineer resume include?

Aim for the 12-15 hard skills that appear across multiple postings for the same title. Padding beyond that reads as keyword stuffing to both parsers and humans.

Does an ATS reject a devops engineer resume automatically?

Most applicant tracking systems rank and filter rather than reject outright, but a low keyword match means a human may never see the resume. The parser is matching strings, not judging experience.

Should I use a PDF or a Word document?

Use .docx unless the posting explicitly asks for PDF. Older parsers still handle Word more reliably.
