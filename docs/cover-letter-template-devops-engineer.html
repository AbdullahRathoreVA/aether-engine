Cover Letter Template for Devops Engineer That Passes ATS

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

When applying for devops engineer positions, it's essential to have a well-structured cover letter that highlights your technical skills and experience. Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter out candidates, so it's crucial to use a template that is optimized for ATS and showcases your qualifications. Here are some key elements to include in your cover letter template:

Introduction and Summary

  • Start with a professional greeting, addressing the hiring manager by name if possible
  • Clearly state the position you're applying for and how you came across the opportunity
  • Write a brief summary of your experience and qualifications, highlighting your most relevant skills and certifications (e.g., AWS Certified DevOps Engineer, Linux Foundation Certified System Administrator)
  • Mention any relevant projects or accomplishments that demonstrate your expertise in devops engineering (e.g., containerization using Docker, continuous integration with Jenkins)

Technical Skills and Experience

  • List your technical skills, including programming languages (e.g., Python, Java), operating systems (e.g., Ubuntu, CentOS), and tools (e.g., Ansible, Puppet)
  • Describe your experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and your ability to manage and deploy applications on these platforms
  • Highlight your knowledge of security best practices and compliance frameworks (e.g., PCI-DSS, HIPAA)
  • Mention any experience with monitoring and logging tools (e.g., ELK Stack, Prometheus) and your ability to troubleshoot and resolve issues

Keywords and Formatting

  • Use keywords from the job description to describe your skills and experience, ensuring that your cover letter is optimized for ATS
  • Use a clear and concise writing style, with bullet points and short paragraphs to make your cover letter easy to read
  • Use a standard font (e.g., Arial, Calibri) and font size (e.g., 10 or 11 points) to ensure that your cover letter is readable on different devices and platforms
  • Save your cover letter in a format that can be easily read by ATS, such as a .docx or .pdf file
