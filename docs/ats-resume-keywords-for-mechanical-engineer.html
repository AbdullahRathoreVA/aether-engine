ATS Resume Keywords for Mechanical Engineer (2026 List)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read
The fastest way to find the right ATS keywords for a mechanical engineer role is to take three live job postings for that exact title and list every hard skill that appears in at least two of them. Those exact strings — spelled the way the posting spells them — belong in your resume wherever you genuinely have the skill.

To increase your chances of passing through applicant tracking systems (ATS) and catching the eye of hiring managers, incorporate relevant keywords into your resume. As a mechanical engineer, focus on technical terms, software, and industry-specific phrases that match the job descriptions you're applying for. Here are some essential ATS resume keywords for mechanical engineers to include in your application materials.

Design and Development Keywords

  • CAD design: Mention specific CAD software such as Autodesk Inventor, SolidWorks, or Catia
  • Product development: Include related terms like prototyping, testing, and validation
  • Mechanical modeling: Use phrases like finite element analysis (FEA) and computational fluid dynamics (CFD)
  • Design for manufacturability (DFM): Highlight your understanding of manufacturing processes and supply chain management

Technical Skills and Tools

  • Programming languages: List languages like Python, C++, Matlab, or Visual Basic
  • Simulation software: Include tools like ANSYS, Abaqus, or OpenFOAM
  • Data analysis and visualization: Mention tools like Excel, Tableau, or Power BI
  • Collaboration platforms: Use phrases like PLM (Product Lifecycle Management) and version control systems like Git

Industry-Specific and Soft Skills

  • Industry standards and regulations: Familiarize yourself with standards like ASME, API, or ISO
  • Safety protocols and procedures: Emphasize your knowledge of OSHA guidelines and risk assessment methodologies
  • Communication and teamwork: Highlight soft skills like project management, leadership, and conflict resolution
  • Continuous learning and professional development: Showcase your commitment to staying up-to-date with industry trends and attending relevant conferences or workshops

Common questions

How many keywords should a mechanical engineer resume include?

Aim for the 12-15 hard skills that appear across multiple postings for the same title. Padding beyond that reads as keyword stuffing to both parsers and humans.

Does an ATS reject a mechanical engineer resume automatically?

Most applicant tracking systems rank and filter rather than reject outright, but a low keyword match means a human may never see the resume. The parser is matching strings, not judging experience.

Should I use a PDF or a Word document?

Use .docx unless the posting explicitly asks for PDF. Older parsers still handle Word more reliably.
