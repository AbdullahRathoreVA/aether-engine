Cover Letter Template for Customer Support That Passes ATS

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of landing a customer support job, you need a well-structured cover letter that showcases your skills and experience. Many companies use applicant tracking systems (ATS) to screen applications, so it's essential to use a template that is optimized for ATS and highlights your customer support skills. Here's a breakdown of what you should include in your cover letter:

Introduction and Summary

  • Start with a formal greeting, addressing the hiring manager by title and last name (if possible)
  • Clearly state the position you're applying for and how you came across the job listing
  • Provide a brief summary of your customer support experience and relevant skills, using keywords from the job description (e.g., customer service software, issue resolution, communication skills)
  • Keep your introduction concise and focused, aiming for 2-3 sentences at most

Body Paragraphs

  • Use 2-3 paragraphs to elaborate on your customer support experience and skills, providing specific examples and anecdotes
  • Focus on the benefits you can bring to the company, such as improved customer satisfaction, increased efficiency, or enhanced issue resolution
  • Use bullet points or numbered lists to break up large blocks of text and highlight your achievements, such as:
    • Resolved an average of 50 customer issues per week with a 95% satisfaction rate
    • Implemented a new customer service process that reduced response times by 30%
    • Collaborated with the product development team to resolve technical issues and improve product quality
  • Use language from the job description to describe your skills and experience, such as problem-solving, active listening, or conflict resolution

Closing and Call-to-Action

  • Express your enthusiasm for the position and the company, and reiterate your interest in the role
  • Include a professional closing, such as a thank-you note or a statement of appreciation for the reader's time
  • Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, and make sure it's easy for the hiring manager to get in touch with you
  • Proofread your cover letter carefully to ensure error-free formatting, grammar, and spelling, as ATS may reject applications with errors
