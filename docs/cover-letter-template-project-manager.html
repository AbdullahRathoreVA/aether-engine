Cover Letter Template for Project Manager That Passes ATS

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of passing through applicant tracking systems (ATS) and catching the eye of hiring managers, your cover letter needs to be tailored to the specific project manager job you're applying for. A well-structured cover letter template can help you showcase your skills and experience in a clear and concise manner. Here's how you can create a cover letter that gets you noticed.

Introduction and Summary

  • Start with a professional greeting, addressing the hiring manager by title and last name (if possible), e.g., Dear Mr. Smith
  • Clearly state the position you're applying for, e.g., Project Manager Position at XYZ Corporation
  • Provide a brief summary of your experience and qualifications, focusing on the most relevant skills and achievements, e.g., Results-driven project manager with 5+ years of experience in Agile methodologies and team leadership
  • Include relevant keywords from the job description to help your cover letter pass through ATS filters

Body of the Cover Letter

  • Describe your experience in managing projects from start to finish, including planning, execution, and monitoring
  • Highlight specific skills and tools you've used in previous roles, such as Asana, Trello, or MS Project
  • Mention any relevant certifications, such as PMP or Agile, and how they've helped you in your career
  • Provide specific examples of successful projects you've managed, including metrics and outcomes, e.g., Successfully managed a team of 10 developers to deliver a software project 20% under budget and 15% ahead of schedule
  • Emphasize your ability to communicate effectively with stakeholders, team members, and clients

Conclusion and Call to Action

  • Summarize your interest in the position and the company, reiterating your relevant skills and experience
  • Express your enthusiasm for the opportunity to discuss your application and how you can contribute to the company's success
  • Include a professional closing, such as Sincerely or Best Regards, followed by your full name and contact information
  • Proofread your cover letter carefully to ensure it is error-free and easy to read, using a standard font such as Arial or Calibri and a font size of 10 or 11 points
