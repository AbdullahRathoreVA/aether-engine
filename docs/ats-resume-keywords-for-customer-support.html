ATS Resume Keywords for Customer Support (2026 List)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of passing the applicant tracking system (ATS) and getting noticed by hiring managers, incorporate relevant keywords into your customer support resume. These keywords are often related to software, skills, and qualifications that are essential for the job. By tailoring your resume to include these keywords, you can improve your visibility and demonstrate your capabilities to potential employers.

Software and Tool Keywords

  • Zendesk: a popular customer support platform used by many companies
  • Freshdesk: a cloud-based customer support software with ticketing and automation features
  • Salesforce Service Cloud: a customer support platform with CRM capabilities
  • HelpScout: a customer support platform with email and chat functionality
  • LiveChat: a live chat and customer support software for websites and applications

Skill-Based Keywords

  • Problem-solving: ability to resolve customer complaints and issues
  • Communication: effective written and verbal communication skills
  • Empathy: ability to understand and relate to customer concerns and emotions
  • Time management: ability to manage multiple customer requests and prioritize tasks
  • Adaptability: ability to adapt to changing customer needs and support environments
  • Technical troubleshooting: ability to troubleshoot technical issues and provide solutions

Qualification and Certification Keywords

  • Customer Service Certification: certification programs that demonstrate customer service skills and knowledge
  • Technical Support Certification: certification programs that demonstrate technical support skills and knowledge
  • HDI Certification: certification programs offered by the Help Desk Institute
  • ITIL Foundation: foundation certification in IT service management
  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or related field: relevant educational background for customer support roles
