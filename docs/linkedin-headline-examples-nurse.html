LinkedIn Headline Examples for Nurse

Updated 24 July 2026 · 2 min read

If you are applying for nurse roles and not hearing back, the bottleneck is usually parsing and keyword match, not your experience.

What to do this week

  • Cross-reference three live job postings for the same title and list every hard skill that appears in at least two of them.
  • Put those exact strings in your skills section, spelled the way the posting spells them. PostgreSQL and Postgres are different tokens to a parser.
  • Rewrite each bullet as action verb + scope + measurable result.
  • Keep the layout single-column. Tables, text boxes, and headers/footers are where parsers lose content.
  • Save as .docx unless the posting explicitly asks for PDF.

How to check your work

Paste your resume and the job description into any AI assistant and ask: “List the hard requirements in this posting that my resume does not mention.” Fix the ones you can honestly claim. Ignore the rest.
