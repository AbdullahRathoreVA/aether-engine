15 Resume Summary Examples for Nurse

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of landing a nursing job, you need a strong resume summary that highlights your skills, experience, and achievements. A well-crafted summary can help you stand out from other applicants and entice hiring managers to read on. Here are some examples of effective nurse resume summaries, categorized by experience level and specialty.

Nurse Resume Summaries for Entry-Level Positions

  • Recent nursing graduate with 90+ GPA, seeking a med-surg position to apply my knowledge of pharmacology and patient assessment.
  • Newly licensed RN with 200 hours of clinical experience, looking for a role in pediatrics or gerontology to utilize my skills in vital signs and medication administration.
  • Entry-level nurse with a strong foundation in anatomy and physiology, eager to work in a fast-paced environment and continue learning from experienced professionals.

Nurse Resume Summaries for Specialized Positions

  • Experienced ICU nurse with 5+ years of experience, skilled in hemodynamic monitoring and critical care, seeking a leadership role to mentor and guide other nurses.
  • Pediatric nurse practitioner with 10+ years of experience, proficient in well-child visits and acute illness management, looking for a position in a community health setting.
  • Operating room nurse with 8+ years of experience, knowledgeable in perioperative care and surgical procedures, seeking a role in a level 1 trauma center to work with a diverse patient population.

Nurse Resume Summaries for Leadership and Management Positions

  • Seasoned nurse manager with 15+ years of experience, skilled in budgeting and staff development, seeking a director-level role to oversee a team of nurses and contribute to hospital-wide initiatives.
  • Assistant director of nursing with 10+ years of experience, knowledgeable in accreditation standards and quality improvement, looking for a position in a large healthcare system to drive positive change and improve patient outcomes.
  • Nurse educator with 20+ years of experience, passionate about curriculum development and clinical instruction, seeking a role in a nursing school to shape the next generation of nurses.
