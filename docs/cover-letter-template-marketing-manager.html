Cover Letter Template for Marketing Manager That Passes ATS

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of landing a marketing manager interview, you need a cover letter that not only showcases your skills and experience but also passes through applicant tracking systems (ATS). A well-crafted cover letter can help you stand out from other applicants and demonstrate your ability to drive marketing campaigns and lead teams. The key is to use a template that highlights your relevant experience and includes the right keywords.

Essential Elements to Include

  • Mention the job title and reference number (if provided) to help the ATS match your application to the job posting
  • Use a formal greeting and address the hiring manager by title (e.g., Dear Hiring Manager) if you don't know their name
  • Clearly state the position you're applying for and how you came across the opportunity
  • Highlight your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment
  • Include relevant marketing certifications, training, or education (e.g., Google Analytics Certification, HubSpot Inbound Marketing)

Keyword Optimization

  • Use keywords from the job posting in your cover letter, especially in the first paragraph (e.g., digital marketing, campaign management, team leadership)
  • Incorporate industry-specific terms and buzzwords (e.g., SEO, social media marketing, content creation)
  • Emphasize your experience with marketing tools and software (e.g., Marketo, Adobe Creative Cloud, Hootsuite)
  • Use action verbs like managed, created, developed, and improved to describe your achievements

Best Practices for Formatting and Style

  • Use a standard font (e.g., Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman) and font size (10 or 11 points)
  • Set the margins to 1 inch on all sides and use a formal business letter format
  • Keep the cover letter concise and focused, ideally no more than 3-4 paragraphs
  • Use bullet points or numbered lists to break up large blocks of text and make your experience stand out
  • Proofread your cover letter multiple times for spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors
