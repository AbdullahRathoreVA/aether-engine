ATS Resume Keywords for Financial Analyst (2026 List)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of getting past applicant tracking systems (ATS) and in front of hiring managers, your resume must contain relevant keywords for the financial analyst position. These keywords are often used by ATS to filter and rank applicants, so including them in your resume is crucial. The following sections outline the most important ATS resume keywords for financial analysts, categorized by skill type.

Technical Skills

  • Financial modeling using tools like Excel, Financial Studio, or Adaptive Insights
  • Data analysis with software such as Tableau, Power BI, or D3.js
  • Accounting principles including GAAP, IFRS, or other relevant standards
  • Budgeting and forecasting methods, including zero-based budgeting or driver-based forecasting
  • Financial reporting tools like Excel, Access, or SQL

Software and Systems

  • Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word, and PowerPoint
  • ERP systems such as SAP, Oracle, or Microsoft Dynamics
  • Financial planning and analysis (FP&A) tools like Anaplan, Host Analytics, or IBM Planning Analytics
  • Data visualization tools including QlikView, SAS Visual Analytics, or Google Data Studio
  • Database management systems like MySQL, PostgreSQL, or MongoDB

Soft Skills and Certifications

  • Communication skills, including written, verbal, and presentation skills
  • Collaboration and teamwork experience, particularly in cross-functional teams
  • Time management and organization skills, with the ability to meet deadlines
  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) certification or progress toward certification
  • CPA (Certified Public Accountant) certification or other relevant accounting certifications
