ATS Resume Keywords for Marketing Manager (2026 List)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read
The fastest way to find the right ATS keywords for a marketing manager role is to take three live job postings for that exact title and list every hard skill that appears in at least two of them. Those exact strings — spelled the way the posting spells them — belong in your resume wherever you genuinely have the skill.

To increase your chances of getting past applicant tracking systems (ATS) and in front of hiring managers, incorporate relevant keywords into your marketing manager resume. These keywords are often pulled from the job description itself and can help demonstrate your qualifications for the position. Here are some essential ATS resume keywords for marketing managers to include in your application materials.

General Marketing Keywords

  • Marketing strategy development and execution
  • Campaign management and analysis
  • Brand management and awareness
  • Digital marketing (including SEO, PPC, email marketing, and social media marketing)
  • Data-driven decision making and metrics analysis
  • Budget planning and management
  • Team leadership and collaboration
  • Market research and competitive analysis
  • Content creation and copywriting

Digital Marketing Keywords

  • Google Analytics and other web analytics tools
  • Facebook Ads and other social media advertising platforms
  • Adobe Creative Cloud (including Photoshop, InDesign, and Illustrator)
  • HubSpot and other marketing automation software
  • SEO optimization techniques (including keyword research and link building)
  • PPC campaign management and optimization
  • Email marketing software (including Mailchimp and Constant Contact)
  • Social media management tools (including Hootsuite and Buffer)

Analytics and Data Keywords

  • Excel and other spreadsheet software
  • SQL and database management
  • Tableau and other data visualization tools
  • Google Data Studio and other reporting software
  • Marketing mix modeling and attribution analysis
  • A/B testing and experimental design
  • Customer lifetime value (CLV) analysis
  • Return on investment (ROI) analysis and calculation

Common questions

How many keywords should a marketing manager resume include?

Aim for the 12-15 hard skills that appear across multiple postings for the same title. Padding beyond that reads as keyword stuffing to both parsers and humans.

Does an ATS reject a marketing manager resume automatically?

Most applicant tracking systems rank and filter rather than reject outright, but a low keyword match means a human may never see the resume. The parser is matching strings, not judging experience.

Should I use a PDF or a Word document?

Use .docx unless the posting explicitly asks for PDF. Older parsers still handle Word more reliably.
