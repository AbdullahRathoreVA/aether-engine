10 AI Prompts for a Software Engineer Job Search

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of landing a software engineer job, you need to leverage AI tools effectively. These tools can help you optimize your resume, practice your coding skills, and even assist with job applications. Here are 10 AI prompts to help you improve your job search strategy and stand out from the competition.

Resume and Online Profile Optimization

  • Use AI to review and improve your resume by asking it to analyze my resume and provide suggestions for improvement or rewrite my resume summary to better match the job description.
  • Ask AI to generate a list of relevant keywords for a software engineer resume to help you tailor your application materials to the job you're applying for.
  • Get AI-powered feedback on your online profiles, such as LinkedIn, by asking it to review my LinkedIn profile and suggest improvements or generate a list of relevant skills to add to my LinkedIn profile.

Coding Skills Practice and Assessment

  • Practice your coding skills with AI-generated challenges by asking it to generate a list of coding challenges for software engineers or create a coding test based on the requirements of the job I'm applying for.
  • Use AI to assess your coding skills and identify areas for improvement by asking it to review my code and provide feedback on best practices or analyze my coding style and suggest improvements.
  • Ask AI to explain common software engineering concepts and provide examples to help you reinforce your understanding of key topics.

Job Application and Interview Preparation

  • Get AI-powered help with job applications by asking it to generate a cover letter for a software engineer job application or assist with writing a follow-up email after applying for a job.
  • Prepare for interviews by asking AI to generate a list of common software engineer interview questions or simulate a mock interview for a software engineer position.
  • Use AI to research the company and position you're applying for by asking it to provide information about the company culture and values or generate a list of questions to ask the interviewer about the company and position.
