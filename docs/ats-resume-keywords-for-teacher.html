ATS Resume Keywords for Teacher (2026 List)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of passing through applicant tracking systems (ATS) and getting noticed by school administrators, incorporate relevant keywords into your teacher resume. These keywords are often related to teaching methods, educational technologies, and specific subjects. Here are some essential ATS resume keywords for teachers to include in their applications.

Core Teaching Keywords

  • Lesson planning (curriculum design, unit planning, assessment development)
  • Classroom management (behavioral management, disciplinary techniques, student engagement)
  • Instructional strategies (differentiated instruction, project-based learning, technology integration)
  • Student assessment and evaluation (formative assessment, summative assessment, data-driven instruction)
  • Parent-teacher communication (parent conferences, progress reports, communication plans)

Subject-Specific Keywords

  • For math teachers: algebra, geometry, calculus, statistics, data analysis
  • For science teachers: biology, chemistry, physics, environmental science, lab management
  • For English teachers: literature analysis, composition, grammar, vocabulary development, reading comprehension
  • For special education teachers: IEP development, accommodations, modifications, behavioral interventions, assistive technology

Educational Technology and Soft Skills

  • Educational software: Google Classroom, Moodle, Blackboard, Canvas
  • Productivity tools: Microsoft Office, Google Docs, Spreadsheets, Presentation software
  • Soft skills: time management, organization, communication, collaboration, problem-solving
  • Other relevant technologies: learning management systems, online grade books, digital portfolios
