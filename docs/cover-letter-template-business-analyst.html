Cover Letter Template for Business Analyst That Passes ATS

Updated 31 July 2026 · 2 min read

To increase your chances of getting hired as a business analyst, you need a cover letter that not only showcases your skills and experience but also passes the applicant tracking system (ATS) filters. A well-crafted cover letter can help you stand out from other applicants and demonstrate your ability to analyze complex business problems. Here's a template to help you get started:

Introduction and Summary

  • Begin with a formal greeting, addressing the hiring manager by title and last name (if possible), e.g. Dear Mr. Smith
  • Clearly state the position you're applying for, e.g. Business Analyst Position at XYZ Corporation
  • Provide a brief summary of your relevant experience and qualifications, e.g. With 5 years of experience in business analysis and a certification in Agile methodologies, I am confident in my ability to drive business growth and improvement

Key Skills and Experience

  • Highlight your technical skills, including proficiency in tools like Excel, SQL, and Tableau, e.g. Proficient in data analysis and visualization using Excel, SQL, and Tableau
  • Emphasize your experience with business analysis methodologies, such as Agile, Waterfall, or hybrid approaches, e.g. Experienced in Agile project management with a focus on iterative development and continuous improvement
  • Mention your understanding of business operations and processes, including experience with process mapping, workflow analysis, and requirements gathering, e.g. Skilled in process mapping and workflow analysis to identify areas for improvement and increase efficiency
  • Include any relevant certifications, such as CBAP, CCBA, or ECBA, e.g. Certified Business Analyst Professional (CBAP) with expertise in business analysis and solution evaluation

ATS-Friendly Formatting and Keywords

  • Use a standard font, such as Arial, Calibri, or Helvetica, and a font size between 10 and 12 points
  • Set the margins to 1 inch on all sides and use a formal business letter format
  • Incorporate relevant keywords from the job posting, such as business analysis, data analysis, process improvement, and project management
  • Use bullet points and white space to make the letter easy to scan and read, e.g. Key strengths include: followed by a bullet point list
  • Save the cover letter in a widely accepted file format, such as PDF or Word document (.docx), to ensure compatibility with ATS systems
